Core Purpose and Operational Areas of the Society

The Mongolian Hydrometeorological Society (MonMetSoc) is a public-serving non-governmental organization dedicated to helping organizations of all ownership types and citizens meet their ongoing needs through advisory services grounded in hydrometeorological and environmental monitoring science, technology, and research data.

Starting in August 2002, D. Tseesodroltsoo, D. Tuvdendorj, R. Oyun, Ts. Adyasüren, and B. Myagmarjav initiated and drafted the society's charter. On September 19, 2002, 17 founding scientists and researchers held the inaugural assembly, approving the charter and officially establishing the society.

5 Core Areas of Activity (Charter Article 2.2):

  • 1. Enhancing Information Utility: Refining methods to deliver timely weather, water, and environmental data services to businesses, herders, and citizens.
  • 2. Commissioned Research & Analysis: Conducting specialized monitoring on microclimates, soil moisture, flood hazards, and pasture ecology upon institutional or public request.
  • 3. Disseminating Scientific Works: Promoting researchers' publications and scientific achievements domestically and internationally, and introducing them into industry practice.
  • 4. Generational Continuity & Professional Growth: Strengthening ties between senior and younger generations, training young specialists, and protecting their collective interests.
  • 5. Multilateral Cooperation: Partnering with governmental and international bodies to implement strategic projects on climate change, desertification, and adaptation.

Chronology & Key Milestones (2002–2026)

A chronological overview based on the Society's archival documents and historical records:

2002.08.03–10
Drafting of the Society Charter

D. Tseesodroltsoo drafted the society's charter and consulted with D. Tuvdendorj, R. Oyun, Ts. Adyasüren, and B. Myagmarjav to convene the founding members.

2002.09.19
Inaugural Founders' Assembly & Board of Directors

At an assembly attended by 17 scientists, a 9-article charter was adopted, and Ts. Adyasüren was elected as the first President by secret ballot with 62.5% of the votes.

Founding Members: N. Luvsanbyambaa, D. Tseesodroltsoo, L. Natsagdorj, Sh. Sanjperlee, D. Tuvdendorj, E. Naidan, D. Chogsom, Ch. Puntsagdorj, L. Jambajamts, Ts. Sürenjav, D. Davaadorj, D. Chuluunbat, D. Khorjav, B. Myagmarjav, Z. Batjargal, Ts. Adyasüren, R. Oyun.
2002.12.12
State Registration Certificate №2801 & 30 Years in Antarctica

Received the official registration certificate from the Ministry of Justice and Home Affairs. On the same day, commemorated the 30th anniversary of the first Mongolian setting foot in Antarctica and published the books "Notes from the Icy Continent" and "Our Tserendeleg".

2003.10.01
“Erdene Shugui” Project (Erdenedalai, Dundgovi)

Under the UN Global Environment Facility Small Grants Programme, planted over 2,900 willows across a 2-hectare plot to combat Gobi-steppe desertification, complete with a deep well and integrated irrigation system.

2003.11.18
Official Society Emblem Approved

From competition entries, artist Sh. Choijil's "Three-Eyed Chandmani Jewel" design was selected and approved as the official emblem of the society.

Spring 2004
Flood Calculations for 22 UBTZ Railway Structures

Successfully introduced an innovative hydrological calculation methodology and early warning forecasts for spring snowmelt floods along the Ulaanbaatar–Darkhan and Salkhit–Erdenet railway lines.

2015–2026
UNDP ADAPT, NAP, JICA & International Projects

Continuously serving as national consultants on Mongolia's National Adaptation Plan (NAP006), rural herder risk management (ADAPT), and Ulaanbaatar air quality management initiatives.

Atmosphere and Meteorological Science

The upward-reaching flame/eye at the apex of the three-eyed Chandmani jewel represents aerospace and atmospheric research—examining the atmosphere enveloping our Earth, weather patterns, winds, storms, precipitation, and climate variability as a whole.

Designer's Note: "When the Buddha attained enlightenment, the third eye was bestowed from the heavens... These three eyes express the concepts of air, water, and earth."

Charter of the Mongolian Hydrometeorological Society (9 Articles)

Adopted at the Founding Assembly on September 19, 2002

1.1. The "Mongolian Hydrometeorological Society" NGO is a public-serving, non-profit, non-governmental organization operating in accordance with the Law on Non-Governmental Organizations and this Charter.

1.2. Official Name: "Монголын ус цаг уурын нийгэмлэг" (abbreviated as МУЦН). English Name: Mongolian Hydrometeorological Society (MonMetSoc).

1.3. Legal Address: Ulaanbaatar 210646, Khudaldaanii Street-5, Building of the National Agency for Meteorology and Environmental Monitoring, Room No. 218. Tel: (+976) 11-312458.

1.8–1.9. The activities and financial reports of MonMetSoc shall be transparent and open to the public, and reported within statutory deadlines.

2.1.1. Purpose: The Society aims to assist organizations of all ownership forms and citizens in making hydrometeorological and environmental monitoring science, technology, and related information a continuous resource.

2.2. Core Operational Areas: Enhancing the utility of information; conducting detailed research upon request; promoting scientists' works domestically and abroad; ensuring continuity between senior and younger generations; and implementing specific research projects commissioned by government and international bodies.

3.1.2. The supreme governing body of the Society shall be a 7-member Board of Directors.

3.1.3–3.1.4. The President of the Society shall be elected at a meeting of the Board of Directors. A member of the Board of Directors may serve as President of the Society.

3.2. Supervisory Council: The Society's Supervisory Council shall consist of 3 members and shall regularly oversee member complaints and financial expenditures.

Powers of the Board of Directors: Amending the Charter, approving budgets and work plans, appointing/dismissing the Executive Director, setting membership fee rates, and conferring Honorary Membership.

Powers of the President: Representing the Society domestically and internationally, chairing Board of Directors meetings, providing unified strategic leadership, and concluding contracts with the Executive Director.

Powers of the Executive Director: Organizing day-to-day operations, managing assets within granted authority, expanding the network of members and supporters, and strengthening financial stability.

Financing (Article 7): Membership dues, donations and support from citizens and enterprises, revenues from charter-compliant research contracts, and funding from international and state projects and programs.

8.1. Honorary Member: By decision of the Board of Directors, Mongolian or foreign citizens who have made substantial contributions or authored significant works in Mongolian hydrometeorology and environmental monitoring may be conferred Honorary Membership.

8.2–8.3. The Society shall maintain its own newsletter, scientific publication series, and official awards such as "Best Researcher of the Year" and "Person of the Year."
